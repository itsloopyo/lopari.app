01 How it works

Surfaces your games. Launches them. Done.

  1. 01

    It finds your games

    Every installed game that supports head tracking - natively, or through a community mod.

  2. 02

    You launch in one click

    Hit play. Lopari adds the mod if a game needs one, and takes it back out when you want vanilla.

  3. 03

    You bring the tracking

    Any head tracker works - a webcam, a phone, whatever you've got.
