How it works

Pick a game. Launch it. Done.

  1. 01

    It finds your games

    Every installed game that supports head tracking - natively, or through an unofficial mod.

  2. 02

    You launch in one click

    Hit play. Lopari adds the mod if a game needs one, and takes it back out when you want vanilla.

You bring the tracker. Lopari launches your games and swaps the mod in and out. The head tracking itself comes from whatever you already use - TrackIR, a Tobii Eye Tracker, or OpenTrack with a webcam or phone.
