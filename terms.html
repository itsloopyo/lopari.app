2. What Lopari is

Lopari is a desktop game launcher. It scans PC games you have lawfully obtained and installed yourself, filters the list to games that support head tracking (whether natively or via a community mod), and gives you a single Play button per game. For a small subset of titles where head tracking is added by a community mod, Lopari can install that mod via its own installer script and uninstall it again via its own uninstaller script. The mods modify only files on your machine and are reversible.
